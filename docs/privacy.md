# Privacy Policy

**PrompterGlass** — effective 2026-08-10

PrompterGlass is a native macOS teleprompter app developed by Victor Castro Contreras ("we", "us").

## The short version

PrompterGlass does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you write stays on your Mac.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your scripts** are saved in a local database on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Your preferences** (overlay position and size, scroll speed, font size, colors) are saved in the standard macOS user defaults, also local to your Mac.

## What we do not do

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No network connections: the app works entirely offline.
- No cookies, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs.

## Data shared with Apple

If you have enabled the macOS option to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may forward anonymized crash reports to us. This is controlled entirely by your macOS privacy settings, not by the app.

## Your data, your control

Deleting a script in the app removes it from local storage. Uninstalling the app removes its container, including all scripts and preferences. Because no data ever leaves your Mac, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and note the change in the project's changelog.
