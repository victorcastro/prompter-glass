# Privacy Policy

PrompterGlass does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your scripts and preferences live only on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container. No analytics, no accounts, no network connections.

The full policy is published at **[victorcastro.github.io/prompter-glass/privacy.html](https://victorcastro.github.io/prompter-glass/privacy.html)** (source: [`docs/privacy.html`](docs/privacy.html)).
