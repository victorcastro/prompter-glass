# Privacy Policy

PrompterGlass does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your scripts and preferences live only on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container. No analytics, no accounts, no network connections. The optional voice-tracking feature transcribes your speech entirely on-device to follow your reading position; audio and transcripts are never stored or transmitted.

The full policy is published at **[victorcastro.github.io/prompter-glass/privacy.html](https://victorcastro.github.io/prompter-glass/privacy.html)** (source: [`docs/privacy.html`](docs/privacy.html)).
